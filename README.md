# 🍽️ Food & Beverage Business Performance Dashboard (Excel)

## 📁 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive *Food & Beverage Business Performance Dashboard* developed using *Microsoft Excel*. 
The dashboard evaluates sales, order composition, and operational efficiency across canteen and café branches for July–August 2025. It analyzes combo vs. single-item sales dynamics, shift-wise ordering habits, branch contributions, and includes a dynamic *Profitability Simulator* to model menu item margins and discount strategies.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard includes the following key visualizations:

1. *Executive Performance KPIs*
   - *Combo Orders:* 86
   - *Combo Orders %:* 24%
   - *Average Ticket Size:* $544.19
   - *Average Gross Profit / Combo:* $278.80
   - *Total Orders:* 360
   - *Overall Gross Margin %:* 51%

2. *Combo vs. Single Item Benchmark*
   - Direct financial comparison table highlighting that combo orders produce a significantly higher average revenue ($544.19 vs. $311.15) and deliver an *Average      Profit of $278.80* compared to single-item purchases.

3. *Combo % in Each Shift*
   - Stacked progress bars comparing combo adoption across operating shifts (*Lunch: 24.55%, **Dinner: 19.18%, **Breakfast: 22.40%*).

4. *Combo % in Each Branch*
   - Comparative bar chart tracking combo conversion rates across facilities: *Plant 2 Canteen (27.69%), **Plant 1 Canteen (11.76%), and **Head Office Café (30.82%)*.

5. *Most Popular Combo Orders*
   - Ranked horizontal bar chart identifying top-performing product pairings (combining Hot Meals, Beverages, Snacks, and Desserts).

6. *Interactive Profitability Simulator*
   - Interactive scenario modeling tool allowing cafeteria managers to select a Hot Meal (e.g., Rajma Chawal), Beverage (e.g., Bottled Water), and input custom Discount % (e.g., 5%) to dynamically compute:
     - *Food Cost:* $94.81
     - *Net Selling Price:* $166.09
     - *Gross Profit:* $71.28
     - *Gross Margin %:* 43%
     - *Current Beverage Stock:* 199 units

---

## 💡 Key Insights
The dashboard reveals the following key operational takeaways:

1. *Combo Value Generation:* While combos represent *24% of total order count (86 orders), they generate a substantially higher ticket size ($544.19) and deliver a robust **51% gross margin*.
2. *Top-Performing Branch:* The *Head Office Café* leads in combo meal adoption at *30.82%, closely followed by **Plant 2 Canteen (27.69%), whereas **Plant 1 Canteen (11.76%)* shows an opportunity for promotion.
3. *Peak Meal Windows:* *Lunch* drives the highest combo sales penetration (*24.55%*), as employees naturally prefer bundled meals during their midday break.
4. *Item Pairing Synergies:* Pairings that bundle a beverage with a hot meal or snack produce the most consistent volume and highest gross profit contribution.

> *Filter Data:* Use the interactive slicers on the right (Branch, Shift) to dynamically segment canteen performance and ticket values.

## 🎯 Final Conclusion & Business Recommendations
* *Incentivize Bundles at Plant 1 Canteen:* Roll out targeted combo promotions at Plant 1 to bring its 11.76% combo conversion rate in line with Head Office (30.8%) and Plant 2 (27.7%).
* *Leverage the Profitability Simulator:* Utilize the built-in simulator before introducing seasonal meal discounts to ensure bundled offerings maintain a gross profit margin of at least 40%–50%.
* *Optimize Lunchtime Prep:* Pre-package high-demand combo items ahead of peak lunch hours to accelerate checkout speeds and increase overall shift throughput.
